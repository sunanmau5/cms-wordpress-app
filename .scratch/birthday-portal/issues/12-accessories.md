# 12 — Activity: birthday accessories cutout

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Simplified mechanic agreed in grilling: accessories sit at **fixed positions** on the cutout photo; a style arrow cycles variants (hat 1/2/3), a swatch row changes colour, a button removes the item. No dragging, resizing or rotating.

- Which accessory slots exist (hat, glasses, moustache, …), how many variants each, and which colours.
- Where the artwork comes from — the user, or generated/sourced.
- Whether the guest can save or share their creation, or it is purely a toy.
- How it degrades if the cutout framing turns out not to suit fixed positions (see 03).
